# KB-005: App crashes and technical problems

1. Update the app to the latest version.
2. Restart the device and reopen the app.
3. Clear the app cache: Settings > Apps > Storage > Clear cache.
4. If the problem continues, uninstall and reinstall the app. Your data is stored in your account and will not be lost.

When contacting support, include the app version, device model, and what you were doing when the problem happened. Screenshots help.
