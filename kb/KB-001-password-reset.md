# KB-001: Resetting your password

If you cannot sign in, use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and enter the email address on your account.

- The reset email arrives within 10 minutes. Check your spam folder.
- The reset link works once and expires after 30 minutes.
- After 5 failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes. Waiting is the only way to unlock it earlier; support cannot unlock it manually.
- If the email never arrives, check that you are using the address the account was created with. Support can confirm the address on file after verifying your identity.
