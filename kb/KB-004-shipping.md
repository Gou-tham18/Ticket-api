# KB-004: Shipping times and tracking

- Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.
- A tracking number is emailed once the order ships. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show updates.
- Orders are processed Monday to Friday. Orders placed after 2 PM, or on weekends, ship the next business day.
- If an order has not arrived 10 business days after shipping, contact support with the order number so a trace can be opened.
- We do not ship to PO boxes.
