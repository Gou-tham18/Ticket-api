# KB-006: Closing your account and exporting your data

- You can export your data from Account > Privacy > Export data. The file is emailed within 24 hours.
- To close your account, go to Account > Privacy > Close account. Closure is permanent after a 7 day grace period.
- Closing an account cancels active subscriptions. It does not automatically refund the current billing period.
- Team accounts can only be closed by the account owner.
